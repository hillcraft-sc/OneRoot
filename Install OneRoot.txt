Installing OneRoot
===================

1. Open OneRoot.dmg (just double-click it).
2. Drag OneRoot onto the Applications folder in the window that opens.
3. Open OneRoot from your Applications folder.

The first time, macOS asks whether you want to open an app downloaded
from the internet. Click Open. OneRoot is signed and checked by Apple,
so that's the only question you'll get.

You can eject the OneRoot disk afterwards and throw away the .dmg.


One more thing: OneRoot needs Full Disk Access to browse all your
files, just like Finder has. On first launch it shows you how to turn
it on: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Full Disk Access, switch
on OneRoot, then relaunch it. You only do this once; updates keep the
permission.
