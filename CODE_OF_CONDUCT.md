# Code of Conduct

OneRoot is a small project and we want it to stay a friendly one.

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith, disagree with ideas rather than people, and keep feedback specific and actionable.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any place you represent the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove comments, close threads, and block people who violate this code. Report problems to admin@hillcraft-sc.com; reports are handled privately.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
